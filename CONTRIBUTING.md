# Helping with jsal.ui

jsal.ui is a free addon that plans your road to 60 in WoW Forever: training, talents, gear, dungeons and
professions in one dashboard. It's made by jsal. It's for **WoW Forever only** (1.60.1).

## Found a bug?

- In game: open the dashboard and pick **Report a problem** at the bottom of its menu, or type `/jsal bug`. It
  adds your version, class, level and any jsal.ui error for you.
- On GitHub: open an issue with the **Report a problem** form.
- If the game showed a Lua error, paste the whole thing, from BugSack or the game's own error window.

## Got an idea?

Open an issue with the **Suggest an idea** form. Say what you'd like and what it would help with while you play.
Or bring it to Discord.

## Sending a pull request

- Keep it small and about one thing. One fix or one feature, not a batch.
- It runs in the game's Lua, which is 5.1. No `//`, `goto`, `<const>` or bit operators.
- Say in the description what a player would notice, and how you checked it in game.
- For anything you can see, add a screenshot.
- Talk about a big change on Discord or in an issue first, so you don't build something that can't be taken.

## What won't be taken

- Support for other versions of WoW (Retail, Classic Era, Cataclysm and the rest). jsal.ui is Forever only.
- Code, art or profiles copied from another addon.
- Saving or swapping gear sets. jsal.ui plans your gear; it doesn't save what you're wearing.
