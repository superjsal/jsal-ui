# jsal.ui

From level 1 to raid night in WoW Forever, planned. Free, from [jsal.io](https://jsal.io/jsal-ui/).

![jsal.ui's dashboard](https://jsal.io/img/jsal-ui/store/cards-0362/01-home.jpg)

## What's in it

- **Plan:** talents, training, gear and dungeons, level by level.
- **Craft:** the cheapest road for your professions, and orders with your guild.
- **Play:** a journal of your character, daily games, and a pal to raise.

Everything is in one dashboard, and every part has its own switch.

## Get it

Install it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/jsal-ui) or [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/jsal-ui), log in, and type `/jsal`. A welcome shows you round the first time. No other addon is needed.
