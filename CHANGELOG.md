# jsal.ui 0.3.10

- **Training fixes:** spells you already know no longer show as ready to train, and a hunter's pet is offered the next rank it can learn (Rank 1 before Rank 2) at the right training-point cost.
- **Quests on autopilot** now starts off. If you'd like quests taken and handed in for you, switch it on in Settings.
- **Dashboard:** text is one size larger, and Home shows a short "New in" note after each update.
- **Training Planner:** your nearest class trainer, with a button to show them on the map. It's also on the quick buttons now.
- **Flight bar:** stays at the top centre, with buttons for Daily Word, Icon Guess and chess.
- **Professions:** hover a step on the road to the next rank to see everything it needs, with what's in your bags and bank. Reaching a new rank now shows a banner naming the nearest trainer; click it to show them on the map.
