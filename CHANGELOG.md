# jsal.ui 0.3.12

- **Journal (new):** your character's story, from the dashboard or `/jsal journal`, in four tabs.
  - **Summary:** your character, your favourite move, and your totals: foes defeated, quests, deaths, gold earned and where it came from, distance, damage and healing, duels and jumps, with the spells you use most and the zones you spend most time in. Kills, quests, deaths, gold and duels come from the game's own statistics, so they cover your character's whole life.
  - **Story:** the moments worth keeping, newest first: levels, first kills of rares, dungeon bosses, blue and epic loot, deaths and what caused them, profession ranks and duels.
  - **By level:** time, XP, XP per hour, gold in and out, kills, quests and deaths for each level.
  - **Characters:** every character on your account with level, zone, gold, professions and rested XP, which keeps growing while they're logged out.
  - A screenshot at each level-up, and a recap of your session as you log out (with a reminder when you're not resting). Both can be turned off under the Journal in Settings.
- **Guild crafters (new):** on the Professions page, search for what you need and see which guildmates can make it, online first, with a button to whisper them. Item tooltips name the guild's crafters too. Guildmates appear once they've opened their profession windows with this version.
- **Report a problem (new):** at the bottom of the dashboard menu, or `/jsal bug`. Say what went wrong and send it; your version, class, level, zone and any jsal.ui error are added for you.
- **Quick buttons:** a Layer button shows the layer you're in (click it to tell your guild), and Settings now has a switch for each button on the bar.
- **Layers (testing):** `/jsal layer` says which copy of a zone you're in, read from the creatures around you. Compare with a guildmate in the same zone and tell us what you see.
