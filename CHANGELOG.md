# jsal.ui 0.3.56

**Professions**

- The road to your next rank is the cheapest way up by what its materials cost. It no longer counts on selling what you make, so Leatherworking past 200 uses Thick Leather instead of piles of Turtle Scale armour.
- The road doesn't send you to learn or buy a recipe for just a few points, or switch to something else for a few crafts.

**Training**

- Skipping a spell's rank also skips the ranks after it, since a trainer won't sell them without it. Unskip one and the rank before it comes back too.
- On a second hunter, pet trainer ranks your other hunter has learned no longer show as ready before their level.

**Gear plan**

- Weights lists every stat WoWSims weighs, so you can change any of them: each school's spell damage, spell penetration, haste, expertise and more.
- Retribution paladins get new weights from WoWSims' Stat Weights tool. Hit gear no longer ranks far too high.

## In 0.3.55

Coming from 0.3.54 or earlier, restart the game after updating, not just /reload. 0.3.55 adds new pictures, and until you restart, corners stay square.

**New look**

- Buttons, cards, tabs, filters and switches have rounded corners, and many of them are now pills.
- The panels and buttons around the game screen are rounded too.
- jsal.ui's windows fade out when they close, the same way they open.
- The dashboard is wider and draws bigger on small screens.

**Pages**

- **Home:** a card for your character at the top, with cards under it for your talents, gear, professions, dungeons, guild and games.
- **Journal:** the Summary uses the whole window, the Story lets you pick a day or a kind of moment, By level shows your best and worst levels, and each of your characters gets a card.
- **Games:** each daily game is a card, with how your guild did today underneath.
- **Guild:** today's best players sit beside the member list.
- **Build Planner:** shows your next talent point and what it does.
- **Training Planner:** what to train now is a card, pet abilities are shown as icons, and your trainer visits up to 60 are drawn as a road.
- **Professions:** what to make next and what you still need are cards, and the steps to the rank's cap are a road.
- **Settings:** split into tabs, one group at a time.
- **Report a problem:** more room to write.

**Dungeon guide**

- Each boss is a card that shows its loot, how often it drops and whether it's an upgrade for you.
- Quests that share steps are one chain now, not two cards.

**Smaller changes**

- Clicking a page's menu entry again closes its window.
- The shopping list is easier to read, and you can change a count with plus and minus.
- Guild Crafts orders are cards with a progress track, and there's a Back button in Crafters.
- The profession window's Filter menu can hide the skill-up numbers.
- Gear plan and Crafting profit explain their numbers under "About these numbers".
- Looking at a suggested build doesn't make you follow it anymore.
- A pet that's away keeps its last training points count.

**Faster**

- The dashboard and planners open without the small stutter they used to have.

**Data**

- Updated from the newest Forever build, plus fresher quest, trainer and vendor data from players.
- New stat weights for hunters, mages and warlocks.

**Removed for now**

- Chess is gone from the menu. Your games and records are kept, so ask in guild if you want it back.
