# jsal.ui 0.3.57

- A new welcome the first time you log in, with a switch for each part, and every part switched on once for everyone. /jsal welcome opens it again.
- Games: the Guild page is now on Games, with the Guild Word as a fourth game and a countdown to the next day's games. Point at a guildmate to see how their day went.
- Gear plan: about 1,100 more pieces, with most of what the level 53 to 60 dungeons drop.
- The dashboard has an Unlock button beside Reload, and jsal.ui has a Discord: /jsal discord.
- Guild Crafts: an order is marked sent once you mail it or trade it over, and asking shows the item or enchant you get.
- And a few smaller fixes.
