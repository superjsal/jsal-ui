# jsal.ui 0.3.57

- A new welcome the first time you log in, with a switch for everything jsal.ui puts on your screen. /jsal welcome opens it again.
- Every part is switched on, once, for everyone. Switch off what you don't want in the welcome or in Settings.
- Gear plan: about 1,100 more pieces, with most of what the level 53 to 60 dungeons drop.
- The dashboard has an Unlock button beside Reload, and jsal.ui has a Discord: /jsal discord.
- Guild Crafts: an order is marked sent once you mail it or trade it over, and asking shows the item or enchant you get.
- And a few smaller fixes.
