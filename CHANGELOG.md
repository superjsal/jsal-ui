# jsal.ui 0.3.62

Restart the game for this one.

- Level 60: a new Endgame page with your gear goal, your raid nights and what to bring, and the Journal turns to bosses, lockouts and raid nights.
- Dungeon guide: every dungeon listed down the left, boss cards kept to their four best drops, and a Trash card for what a dungeon's ordinary creatures drop.
- Flight bar: flights that pass through other towns are much closer to the real time. A first flight on a long route can still be a few seconds out; after that it's timed from your own flight. Thanks, cloudy2k/frickin packet, for finding it.
- Quest rewards: an arrow on every reward that's an upgrade, gold on the best.
- And a few smaller fixes.
