# jsal.ui 0.3.58

- Games: three cards across, with the Guild Word under Daily Word and your guild beside it.
- Dungeon guide: a Map tab, with each boss marked on the dungeon's own map, for 19 of the classic dungeons and raids. Seven of Forever's own dungeons have a map too, without boss marks. A boss that drops an upgrade for you, or a piece you starred, is marked on the map, and so is one a quest in your log needs. Point at a boss on the map for its picture, what it drops for you and the quests that need it. The mouse wheel zooms it.
- Officers can set the Guild Word too, not only the guild master.
- Unlock Mode shows each piece as itself where it was a gold block, so you can see what you're placing.
- Journal: by level, the first record is your best pace (the most XP an hour), where the fastest level was always an early one.
- Restart the game once for this version: it adds new files.
- And a few smaller fixes.
