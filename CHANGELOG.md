# jsal.ui 0.3.7

- **Quick buttons:** a small bar you can drag anywhere, with buttons for the dashboard, Daily Word, Icon Guess, chess, the shopping list and the Build Planner. A gold dot shows when something's waiting, like today's puzzle or your move in chess. Right-click its grip to pick the buttons, stand it up and down, or lock it.
- **Minimap button:** right-click it for the same places.
- **Shopping list** (`/jsal shop`): what you mean to craft and buy, less what's already in your bags and bank, with vendor prices and auction prices from your own searches. Add a recipe from its details, or a whole profession road at once. A vendor who sells something on it offers to buy it for you.
- **Profession window:** where each recipe turns yellow, green and grey; reagents in your bags and bank; a timer while you craft several; recipes you haven't learned yet, with a star for the ones you want (a profession trainer then shows which it can teach); and a heads-up when you can train the next rank.
- **Settings:** now two columns that fit on one screen. Hover a part to see what it does; parts with options have a Settings button.
- **New icons** drawn for jsal.ui across the dashboard and the quick buttons.
- **Daily Word** starts over at puzzle #1.
- **Build Planner:** Wowhead links you export now open with the talents in the right order.
