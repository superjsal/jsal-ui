# jsal.ui 0.3.63

Restart the game for this one.

- Professions: Fishing has its own tile, with the nearest water where no fish get away from you and your nearest trainer.
- Horde characters are sent to Horde trainers, vendors and dungeons, and are no longer offered Alliance-only patterns, portals or gear.
- Training Planner: your nearest trainer is one who teaches the spells you need, in your starting area or town, not always a capital.
- Gear plan: one spec picker at the top sets both your weights and your At 60 set.
- Quest rewards: when a quest lets you choose a reward, the biggest upgrade for you wears a gold crown, in the quest window, the quest log and the map.
- Everything in this version: jsal.io/jsal-ui/updates
